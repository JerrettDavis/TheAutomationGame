# Industries and Campaign

## Campaign strategy

Each industry introduces concerns that naturally emerge from its work. Industries should reuse the same ontology and systems while emphasizing different constraints.

## Proposed progression

### 1. Restaurant / food service

Teaches:

- manual process;
- queues;
- bottlenecks;
- layout;
- training;
- basic equipment;
- simple quality;
- human improvisation.

Candidate jobs:

- dishwasher;
- busser;
- prep cook;
- cashier;
- shift lead.

### 2. Warehouse

Teaches:

- receiving;
- routing;
- inventory;
- scanning;
- batching;
- concurrency;
- exception queues;
- physical automation.

### 3. Retail / convenience store

Teaches:

- customer interaction;
- point-of-sale;
- payments;
- peripherals;
- promotions;
- refunds;
- degraded/offline operation;
- shift handoff.

### 4. Factory

Teaches:

- production lines;
- throughput;
- maintenance;
- quality control;
- changeover;
- feedback loops;
- upstream/downstream coupling.

### 5. Logistics network

Teaches:

- multiple facilities;
- scheduling;
- transport;
- eventual consistency;
- vendor contracts;
- forecasting;
- disruption.

### 6. Healthcare or similarly safety-critical operation

Teaches:

- authorization;
- audit;
- human judgment;
- escalation;
- competing priorities;
- safety constraints.

Content must be fictionalized enough to avoid presenting the game as professional clinical guidance.

### 7. Financial/transactional organization

Teaches:

- invariants;
- idempotency;
- reconciliation;
- security;
- fraud/risk tradeoffs;
- auditability.

### 8. Software/platform/data center

Teaches:

- services;
- deployment;
- observability;
- dependency graphs;
- capacity;
- versioning;
- contracts;
- platform engineering.

By this point the player recognizes software systems as another kind of operational system rather than a separate abstract universe.

## Cross-industry transfer

Capabilities discovered in one industry should reappear elsewhere:

- queue management;
- inspection;
- authorization;
- routing;
- scheduling;
- identity;
- notification;
- maintenance;
- reconciliation.

This reinforces abstraction through lived repetition.

## Sandbox

Campaign scenarios teach through curated conditions. Sandbox mode lets players create or import arbitrary organizations and industries using the same primitives.

Long-term, sandbox is the retention engine.
